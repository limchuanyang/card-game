1. Each player is dealt 13 cards.

3. Click card to select, click Play to submit. If your selection doesn't match table combo, a warning popup explains why.
4. Clear all cards to win!

How to play UNO

Each player starts with 7 cards. Play a card that matches the colour or symbol of the top card. ⊘ skips the next player, ⇄ reverses direction, +2 makes the next player draw two. ★ and +4 are wild: pick a colour (+4 also makes the next player draw four). Click a card once to select it and again to play it. No playable card? Draw; pass if you still cannot play. First to empty their hand wins.

How to play Rummikub

Each player starts with 14 tiles. Lay down sets: a group (3–4 tiles of the same number in different colours) or a run (3+ consecutive numbers in one colour). ☺ is a joker. Your first set must total 30+ points. After that you can lay more sets or add selected tiles to a set on the table. Cannot play? Draw a tile. First to empty their rack wins. This version does not allow rearranging sets already on the table.
